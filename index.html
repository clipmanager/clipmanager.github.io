Clip Manager

A Windows desktop app that turns your own videos and live streams into short clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels – processed locally on your own computer.

Desktop-App für Windows: erstellt aus eigenen Videos und Streams kurze Clips – lokal auf dem eigenen Computer.

What Clip Manager does

  • Finds highlights in your own stream recordings (game events, reactions, chat activity).
  • Cuts, frames and subtitles the clips in vertical 9:16 format.
  • Optionally uploads a clip you selected to your own TikTok or YouTube account – only when you click the button, directly from your computer.

Clip Manager runs entirely on your computer. We do not operate servers for the app and do not collect analytics.
